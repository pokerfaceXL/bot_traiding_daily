   FALSIFIED for this name.

   +875 / shorts −58; all big winners long), but long-only worsens monthly regularity (7→9/12)
   because shorts hedged some long-losing months. Direction axis FALSIFIED for the regularity
   goal.

   sizing refinement cannot address a basket-wide regime. The only licensed next step is a
   separately pre-registered **causal regime signal** (portfolio-level veto/weight, no
   look-ahead; protocol §8 regime filter / §13), defined before implementation — not launched
