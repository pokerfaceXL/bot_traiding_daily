## 1. Method

### 1.1 Cost model (engine identity)

Harness (`costs.py` / `equity.py`) charges, per trade:

- commission on entry notional **and** exit notional,
- half-spread on entry notional **and** exit notional,
- slippage **once** on entry notional.

So engine round-trip bps ≈ `2·commission + 2·half_spread + slippage` (not `2·(c+h+s)`).
The research convention “17 bps per side → 34 bps RT” is the *event-study / decision* bar;
stored Train-1 blotters were run at **10 / 5 / 2**, i.e. ≈ **32 bps RT** in the engine sense.
Reprice reconstructs `net = gross − new_costs + funding_pnl` from stored blotters (identity
checked to &lt;1e−13 USD on Donchian, funding-carry, and OI-fade).

Notional: **stake = $100**, leverage = 1 → **$100** entry notional (catalog default).

Symbols (harness `SYMBOLS`): **BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, XRPUSDT, DOGEUSDT**.

### 1.2 Spread / impact measurement

**Historical L2:** `https://public.bybit.com/` exposes `trading/`, `spot/`, `premium_index/`,
`spot_index/`, `kline_for_metatrader4/` only. Paths `orderbook/` and `orderBook/linear/` return
404. **No free historical Bybit L2 snapshot archive was found** (probed 2026-10-06).

**Live L2 (REST sanity check):** `GET /v5/market/orderbook?category=linear&limit=50` for all
five symbols. Quoted half-spread and book-walk impact for $100 notional.

**Trade-tape proxy (Train-1 sample):** for each of 10 calendar days spread across Train-1
(incl. high-vol 2024-03-15, 2024-08-05, 2024-11-11), download one
`public.bybit.com/trading/<SYM>/<SYM><day>.csv.gz`, reduce, **delete immediately**. Disk guard:
abort if free space on `/home/limen` &lt; 20 GB (observed free ≈ 31 GB throughout; peak use
transient). Proxy: EMA mid (span 20 trades); adverse `side·(price−mid_pre)/mid` in bps =
effective half-spread; also Roll half-spread on 1-minute last prices.

**OHLC backup:** Corwin–Schultz half-spread on BTC/ETH 5m frames and on hourly caches for
SOL/XRP/DOGE (hourly CS is noisy/upward-biased — reported for context only, **not** used as
the adopted cost).

**Adopted measured basket (equal-symbol median of trade-proxy medians):**

| component | median | p90 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| half-spread (trade effective) | **0.53 bps** | **1.67 bps** |
| impact beyond touch @ $100 (live walk) | **≈0.03 bps** | ≈0.03 bps (single live snapshot) |

Official VIP0 fees (Bybit Trading Fee Structure, USDT perp): **5.5 bps taker / 2.0 bps maker**.
Owner’s actual tier TBD — three commission scenarios kept until then. **Maker is an optimistic
bound only** (fills not guaranteed for these signal styles).

## 6. Caveats

1. No free historical L2 — impact is live-only at $100; path-dependent adverse selection
   during entries is not measured.
2. Trade-effective spread ≠ exchange BBO; may understate in one-sided bursts.
3. Maker 2 bps is optimistic; these strategies are typically taker-at-signal.
4. Owner fee tier unknown — keep 10 / 5.5 / 2 commission scenarios.
5. Engine RT (slip once) ≠ research “2× per-side” bar; both reported.
6. Concurrent F012 docs may land on main; this commit is docs + audit artifacts only.
7. Best-tested *variants* (gates/sizing) were not fully re-blotted here when no separate
   trade CSV existed; baselines above are the autopsy NO_TRAIL Train-1 ledgers that underpinned
   FREEZE. Variant means move nearly 1:1 with the same cost delta.
